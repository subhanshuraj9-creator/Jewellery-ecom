# Art of Light — image-generation prompts

All assets used the built-in image-generation tool. Existing source photographs were supplied through `referenced_image_paths`; no original asset was overwritten. The output/source mapping is in `art-light-assets.json`.

## Product edit template

One call per product, replacing `{name}` with `solstice-ring`, `lune-hoops`, `orbit-necklace`, `cuff`, `signet`, `pearl-earrings`, `chain` or `pendant` and supplying `public/images/{name}.webp` as the edit target:

> Edit target: the supplied {name}.webp is the exact fictional AURELLE jewellery product. Preserve its design, geometry, number of pieces, relative proportions, clasps, pearls, chain and details exactly. Create a high-end luxury product photograph of this SAME jewellery, isolated on a near-black warm espresso studio surface (#15120e), subtly reflective dark stone. Warm champagne gold with controlled bright softbox reflections from upper left, deep believable contact shadows, cinematic directional light, premium photographic realism. Keep the complete object inside the frame, centered, with 15% breathing room on each edge. For necklaces preserve the entire complete chain loop and pendant inside the frame. Square image. No lettering, no logos, no extra jewels, no unrelated props, no hands. Background must be dark, not beige. This is a portfolio concept product shot.
