## Bracelet collection portrait

Saved as `public/images/muse-bracelet-portrait.webp`, generated with the built-in image generation tool and optimized with Sharp. Reference inputs were `muse-ring-portrait.webp` for campaign identity and `cuff.webp` for the bracelet. This is generated concept artwork. The original cuff still life remains the collection hover detail.

> Create one photorealistic luxury jewellery campaign portrait for the fictional AURELLE collection. Reference image 1 establishes the exact adult model identity, black tailoring, natural skin texture, slicked-back dark hair, charcoal grey studio background and restrained neutral lighting. Reference image 2 establishes the exact bracelet design: a smooth polished yellow-gold open cuff, tapering between a narrow rounded end and a wider organic paddle end, without stones, engravings or clasp. Preserve the same woman's facial identity. Compose a vertical 4:5 editorial photograph with her complete face in the upper half, natural brown eyes, quiet expression, and one forearm raised gently beside her shoulder and jaw so the wrist and cuff are clearly visible in the central lower half. Her relaxed hand should sit beside rather than obscure her face. Anatomically plausible wrist, hand, five fingers, no duplicated limbs. The cuff must sit naturally around the wrist with realistic contact, reflections and shadows; show enough of its smooth asymmetric form to identify it with the product reference. Black tailored blazer, small simple gold hoop earrings only, no necklace and no rings. Soft large studio key light from camera left, subtle falloff, natural pores, credible fashion photography, sophisticated espresso and muted ivory colour grade, no artificial skin smoothing. Leave breathing room around face and wrist for a tall collection crop. No text, logo, borders, collage or extra objects. The result should feel like another photograph from the same campaign as reference 1.

Generated using the **built-in image generation tool**, not the API/CLI fallback. All nine final assets are stored in `public/images/` as WebP. Generated PNG source images remain in the local generation history; optimized project copies are self-contained and do not reference that history.
